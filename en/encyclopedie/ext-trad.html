Dante Trad extension

A tab on the side of Firefox that turns Dante Trad's live translation on or off, without leaving the video.

Dante Trad subtitles any video playing on the phone in French, offline. The extension adds a small tab in Firefox to start it with one tap while you watch.

The three Dante extensions sit in the same rail: dragging one tab moves the whole rail.
